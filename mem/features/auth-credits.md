- Public customer registration uses a valid email, chosen nickname (no real name), and password of at least 8 characters. No Google or social sign-in.
- Customers must verify their email before using protected features, sending messages, or buying credits.
- Each new account receives exactly five free sent messages, tracked durably on the server; partner replies are free. Paid credits allow continued chatting.
- Exhausted credits block the input and show package choices with exact prices and message quantities.
